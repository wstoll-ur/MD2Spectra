# Models and references

- [FAIR Chemistry / UMA](https://github.com/facebookresearch/fairchem): forces
  and energies. Obtain weights through the authors' gated
  [Hugging Face repository](https://huggingface.co/facebook/UMA) and observe its
  model license. Use the authors' current citation guidance.
- [MACE-MDP](https://github.com/Nilsgoe/MACE-MDP): molecular dipoles and
  polarizabilities. Gönnheimer, Reuter, Kapil and Margraf, *MACE-MDP: A General
  Dipole and Polarizability Model for Organic Molecules and Materials*.
  See the upstream citation and Academic Software License. Automatic download
  pins upstream commit `91ca3140b25a272aad79a8baece2563c166fd766` and checkpoint
  SHA-256 `126f8d1602549e6fa0df775c701a5119ddeb0e3738202af8e7aa736de6c2b692`.
- [ASE](https://ase-lib.org/): CIF reading, molecular dynamics, geometry and
  trajectory I/O. Bussi, Donadio and Parrinello, *Canonical sampling through
  velocity rescaling*, J. Chem. Phys. 126, 014101 (2007),
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2408420).
- [SciPy Welch estimator](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.signal.welch.html):
  windowed power spectral estimation.

Model weights, upstream source copies, experimental papers and deposited
crystallographic datasets are not bundled. Dependency/model licenses remain
separate from this project's code. The synthetic example CIF is generated by
`examples/make_example.py` and is not an experimental structure.
